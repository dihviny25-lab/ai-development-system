# Decision Log

Use this log for decisions that future agents/engineers would otherwise have to rediscover.

Do not record trivial implementation details.

## Template

### DEC-000 — [Decision title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD  
**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded | deprecated

**Context**

What problem/constraint forced a decision?

**Decision**

What was chosen?

**Alternatives considered**

- Option A — why not.
- Option B — why not.

**Consequences**

Positive and negative consequences.

**Evidence / references**

Issue, PR, measurement, documentation, or experiment.
